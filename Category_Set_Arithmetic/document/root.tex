\title{Arithmetic and Primitive Recursion in the Category of Sets}
\author{Dustin Bryant}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  Building on the entry \emph{Category\_Set}, which formalises the
  Elementary Theory of the Category of Sets (ETCS), we develop
  elementary arithmetic on the natural numbers object. Starting from
  the successor function, we construct addition, a truncated
  subtraction (monus), the usual linear order, multiplication, and
  exponentiation, together with their standard algebraic and
  order-theoretic properties. We also construct the integers as a
  quotient of pairs of naturals equipped with addition and
  multiplication, and define finite (Sigma) summation. Finally, we
  provide a general scheme for primitive recursion and iteration on
  the natural numbers object, along with tooling for defining new
  recursively specified functions from it, which is used extensively
  by the companion entry on finite and countable sets.
\end{abstract}
